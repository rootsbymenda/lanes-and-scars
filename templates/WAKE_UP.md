# Wake

## The order

1. Say the date you are running on. The board's dates mean nothing without today's.
2. Read `SWITCHBOARD.md` in full. It is one line per lane. If it has grown past that, say so: a fat switchboard is a defect.
3. Report the board: one line per lane. Never "what are we working on?"; the board says what we are working on.
4. Wait for the human to name the lane. Then load `lanes/{lane}.md`: the head and the pickup note. Nothing else.
5. Load rules on touch, not at wake. When the work enters an area (deploy, money, ...), open `MEMORY.md`, find the area's folder, list it, and read the files whose names apply.
6. When the human says the window is closing, run `CLOSE_CHECKLIST.md`.

## What not to do at wake

- Do not load every lane "to be safe". The human routes.
- Do not summarise the lanes from memory. Read the file.
- Do not treat a lane's line as the receipt. It points to the lane file, which points to the work.
