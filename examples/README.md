# An invented example

Nora is a freelance translator, German to English. She works with Claude in Claude Code. Two lanes: `client-glossaries` and `website`.

Everything in `translator/` is invented: Nora, her clients, the dates, the session ids, the transcript lines, the sessions themselves. No real person or business is behind any of it. The files are shown as they would stand after a few months of use.

Read in this order: `translator/CLAUDE.md`, `translator/WAKE_UP.md`, `translator/SWITCHBOARD.md`, then `translator/one-session.md`, then the lanes and the three scars.
