# "Later" means park it in the lane and stop asking.

Invented example. Area: behaviour · Earned: 2026-04-21 · Lane: website

## The words

"later means later. write it in the lane and stop asking me. I will tell you when. every time you bring it up I lose the thread I was on." — Nora, 2026-04-21

## The incident

Three sessions in a row, Claude opened with a suggestion to replace the mailto link with a contact form. Nora had said "later" the first time. The second time she said "not now". The third time she stopped the session to say the words above. The suggestion was not wrong; raising it again was.

## Why

Nora decides the order of the work. A parked item is a decision, not an open question. Raising it again asks her to decide again, and costs her the attention she was giving to the lane she chose. The lane file is the place that remembers it, so that Claude does not have to.

## How to apply

- When Nora says "later", "not now", "park it", or the like: write the item under Parked in the lane's head, with the date and her words. Then drop it.
- Do not raise a parked item. Not at wake, not at close, not as "by the way".
- It comes back only when Nora names it, or when she asks "what is parked?".
- If a parked item blocks the current work, say that once, in one line, and let her rule.
